# Clickability

**Principle:** users should never have to hunt for what they can click or tap, or guess what a control will do.

| ID | Check | How to check |
|---|---|---|
| K1 | Everything clickable looks clickable | Buttons look like buttons. Links differ from body text by more than colour alone |
| K2 | Nothing unclickable looks clickable | No blue underlined headings, no button-shaped labels, no card that looks tappable but is not |
| K3 | One clear primary action per screen | The main button is visually stronger than every other button |
| K4 | Destructive or secondary actions are weaker and set apart | "Cancel", "Reset" and "Delete" do not sit beside the main action in the same style |
| K5 | Button and link text says what happens | "Create account", not "Submit". "Download invoice", not "Click here" |
| K6 | Standard controls are used | Real links, buttons, checkboxes, selects. Not a styled `div` or `span` with a click handler |
| K7 | Conventions are followed | Logo top-left links home. Search is a box with a button or magnifier. Cart is a cart |
| K8 | Controls give feedback | Hover, pressed, focus, loading and disabled states are visible |
| K9 | Tap targets are big enough and spaced apart | About 44 by 44 px on touch screens, with room between neighbours |
| K10 | Nothing depends on hover alone | Menus, tooltips and actions revealed on hover are reachable by tap and keyboard |
