seo-desktop

A Mac app that crawls any website into a local database so AI agents can work on it. The first feature finds internal links, tells the agent exactly where to add them, and checks that they went live.

06 · Internal linking

Life of one suggestion

Audit · free tier, no AI

  • Orphan pages and click depth
  • Broken links, redirect chains, nofollow
  • Generic or repeated anchors
  • Internal PageRank
  • Compared with LinkStorm's 2.5M-link study

Suggestions · paid

  • Targets: orphans, deep pages, few links, tagged priority
  • Matched by meaning against the target's title and H1
  • Skips existing links, even through redirects or canonicals

08 · Fast and light

Budgets the tests enforce

These are first estimates. The opening benchmark replaces them with measured numbers.

MeasureBudget
App memory while crawling< 300 MB
Hidden browser, only while rendering< 400 MB
CPU time to clean one page< 10 ms
Stored HTML per page< 15 KB
Recrawl of 10,000 unchanged pages< 3 min
Scoring 200,000 paragraph vectors< 30 s

How it stays cool

  • Background work runs on the efficiency cores only
  • Meaning vectors are made only when a module needs them
  • On battery: one request at a time. In Low Power Mode: paused

What it can't speed up

The website sets the pace. With Crawl-delay: 10, a 50,000-page site takes about 6 days the first time. Later runs are quick because most pages haven't changed.

10 · Scope

Built first, and left for later

Quality gates, built first

  • A 10,000-page test site with a known answer key
  • Shyam's 880-case LinkStorm anchor benchmark: the expected anchor must be in the top 5
  • Hand-checked real pages in Hindi, Japanese, Arabic and German

Later, each with its own spec

  • Licensing and payments
  • WordPress, Webflow and Shopify connectors
  • On-device language mode
  • Google Search Console data

Dropped on purpose

  • Pause and resume (cancel, then crawl again)
  • Cookie login for crawling
  • Auto-accepting suggestions (the agent decides)
